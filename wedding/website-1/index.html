The Bahaghara rituals

A short guide for our guests to the moments of an Odia wedding.

First

Barajatri ବରଯାତ୍ରୀ

The groom and his family arrive at the wedding venue in a grand, celebratory procession and are welcomed by the bride's family.

Then

Kanyadana କନ୍ୟାଦାନ

The father places his daughter's hand in the groom's hand, formally giving her away.

Then

Hasta Granthi & Lajahoma ହସ୍ତଗ୍ରନ୍ଥି

Instead of the usual pheras, the couple's hands are tied together with mango leaves, and together they offer puffed rice and sacred grains into the holy fire.

Then

Kaudi Khela କଉଡ଼ି ଖେଳ

A fun game: the bride holds a sacred cowrie shell (kaudi) tightly in her fist, and the groom must pry it open using only his little finger.

Then

Sala Bidha ଶଳା ବିଧା

A lighthearted moment — the bride's brother gives the groom a playful punch on the back, a reminder to take good care of his sister.

Finally

Sasu Dahi Pakhala Khia ଶାଶୂ ଦହି ପଖାଳ ଖିଆ

The groom sits on his mother-in-law's lap and is playfully fed curd-rice (dahi pakhala) by her.
